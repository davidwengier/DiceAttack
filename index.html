A LITTLE KNOWLEDGE. A LITTLE LUCK.

How to defy the darkness.

01 One die. Endless possibilities. Start with one Base Attack die. Roll your whole collection each turn; each die automatically performs its own effect. Duplicate dice each roll separately. Select a rolled die to reroll it. You get one reroll each turn, or two with the Lucky Fingers skill.

02 Build your collection. Attack dice deal their value as damage, with +3 on a six. Guard blocks; Heal restores health. Venom adds poison, Flame ignores shields, Blood attacks and heals, and Fortune earns gold and blocks. Frost deals half its value, rounded up, through shields and reduces enemy damage by that half-value for this turn. Lightning strikes twice on natural 5s and 6s. Bloom heals half its value, rounded up, and blocks half, rounded down. Hover or select a die for its exact effect.

03 Choose your dice variants. Every type comes in six materials: Base, Gold +2, Diamond +4, Ruby +6, Emerald +8, and Obsidian +10. Bonuses apply before calculating all effects, including half-roll effects. Criticals and Lightning double strikes depend on the natural roll, not the bonus. Open Rare materials in a shop card to see Ruby, Emerald, and Obsidian.

04 Spend your spoils. Every kill opens an animated reward screen showing your gold and health recovery (up to 8 health, improved by skills and omens). Press Continue when ready. Rounds 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 are shops only: no enemy and no combat. Buy multiple copies, up to six dice total. Every shop stocks a Frost, Lightning, or Bloom die. In My dice, sell copies for half their purchase price, rounded down. The free starter refunds 0. Keep at least one damage-dealing die. Abilities refresh each battle; skills last the run. You can also buy healing or refresh stock once per shop.

05 Read your enemy. You heal and attack first, poison then ticks, and surviving monsters act. Shields block normal damage, not Flame, Frost, or poison. Poison stacks up to 12 and loses 1 strength each turn. Monsters gain +1 damage every three turns. Frost reduces damage before your guard blocks it, and wears off after this turn. Frost Seal skips the enemy's next action.

06 No two paths alike. Explore 31 rounds across the Whispering Wilds, Hollow Catacombs, Ember Throne, Frostglass Peaks, Sunken Kingdom, and Astral Rift. Each area has three random encounters, a chapter guardian, and a shop. After the last shop, face the Starless Sovereign on round 31. Complete 25 battles and six shop rounds to win. Chapter omens and merchant stock change each run. Your best record counts completed rounds, including shops. Reset between actions for a fresh run.
