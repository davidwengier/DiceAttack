A LITTLE KNOWLEDGE. A LITTLE LUCK.

How to defy the darkness.

01 Roll your dice. You get three six-sided dice each turn. Select a die and reroll it once per turn if you want to risk a better result.

02 Choose their fate. Click a die, then Attack, Guard, or Mend. Assign multiple dice to the same action. Click an assigned die to move it.

03 Make your move. Attack deals the rolled value as damage; every attack die showing 6 adds +3. Guard blocks its value for this turn. Mend heals its value, up to your maximum health. Blessings improve these values.

04 Read your enemy. The monster shows its next move. Your healing and attack resolve first, then a surviving monster acts. Enemy shields block damage before health. Unused guard disappears at the end of the turn. Monsters gain +1 damage every three turns, so don't wait forever!

05 Go deeper. Beat nine monsters across three chapters. After each victory, recover 8 health and choose a permanent blessing. Defeat the last demon to win.

Keyboard: 1–3 select dice · A attack · G guard · M mend · R reroll · Space roll / fight
