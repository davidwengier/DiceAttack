A LITTLE KNOWLEDGE. A LITTLE LUCK.

How to defy the darkness.

01 Your party. Your turns. Start alone with one Base Attack die. Rescue Mira after the guardian on round 4, and Sol after round 9. Each companion has their own health, shields, dice, abilities, and rerolls. Roll and fight with each living member once; then every surviving enemy acts. You may choose any companion whose turn is ready before rolling. Downed companions cannot act, but recover after the whole battle is won. You lose only when the entire party falls.

02 Build your collection. Attack dice deal their value as damage, with +3 on a six. Guard blocks; Heal restores health. Venom adds poison, Flame ignores shields, Blood attacks and heals, and Fortune earns gold and blocks. Frost deals half its value, rounded up, through shields and reduces enemy damage by that half-value for this turn. Lightning strikes twice on natural 5s and 6s. Bloom heals half its value, rounded up, and blocks half, rounded down. Hover or select a die for its exact effect.

03 Choose your dice variants. Every type comes in six materials: Base, Gold +2, Diamond +4, Ruby +6, Emerald +8, and Obsidian +10. Bonuses apply before calculating all effects, including half-roll effects. Criticals and Lightning double strikes depend on the natural roll, not the bonus. Open Rare materials in a shop card to see Ruby, Emerald, and Obsidian.

04 Equip every companion. Every kill shows its exact gold and capped recovery. A partial victory heals your most injured living ally; winning the whole battle heals everyone and revives downed members. Rounds 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 are shops only. Choose a companion in the shop before buying or selling their dice and abilities. Each can own up to six dice; duplicate dice roll separately. Skills benefit the entire party. Sell dice for half their purchase price, rounded down; each member must keep a damage-dealing die. Abilities reset each battle. You can also buy healing for the selected companion or refresh stock once.

05 Keep your unused shield. Each member stores their own shield. Damage spends only what it needs: 6 shield against 2 damage leaves 4. Guard, Fortune, and Bloom stack with stored shield, which clears at the next battle or shop. Poison ticks before each affected enemy acts, caps at 12, and loses 1 strength per tick. Flame, Frost, poison, and parry counters ignore enemy shields. Frost weakens the chosen enemy for its next action. Frost Seal skips that enemy's action. Enemies gain +1 damage every three full party rounds.

06 Read the whole battlefield. Early battles contain just one enemy. The catacombs introduce pairs; later areas feature groups of one to three. Click an enemy card to choose where attacks, poison, and offensive abilities land. Each enemy has separate health, shield, poison, and intent. Enemy cards show who will be attacked. Dead enemies never act; the round finishes only when the entire group is defeated. Mira heals the most injured living ally; Sol adds +2 damage per offensive die and starts with Frost Seal.

07 Dodge or risk a parry. Each enemy strike opens a reaction popup. Press Ready when prepared. The marker travels from left to right: press Dodge or D inside the broad blue window to avoid damage, or Parry or P inside the narrower gold window to avoid damage and counter for 4 + Ember Edge damage through armor. You get one attempt per strike. Early, late, or missed reactions take normal damage, with your stored shield still protecting you. Take the hit (or Escape) deliberately skips the reaction. Relaxed timing doubles the time available. Switching tabs pauses the timing meter.

08 No two paths alike. Explore 31 rounds across six illustrated areas. Each area has three random battles, a chapter guardian, and a shop. After the last market, face the Starless Sovereign and its escorts on round 31. Complete 25 battles and six shops to win. Your best record counts completed rounds, not individual kills. Reset between actions for a fresh adventure.

Your run and tutorial step save automatically on this browser. Refreshing, closing the page, or switching tabs restores your dice, health, shields, gold, shop stock, and progress. Interrupted animations return to the last completed action, without spending or awarding anything twice. The tutorial pops up on your first visit; replay it with Learn to play. Reset run replaces the saved adventure. Saves are local to this browser, not shared across devices.

Keyboard: 1–6 select dice · R reroll · Space roll / fight · D dodge · P parry · Escape close dialogs (or take the hit during a reaction)
